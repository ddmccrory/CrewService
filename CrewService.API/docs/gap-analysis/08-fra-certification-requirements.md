# 08 – FRA Craft Certification Requirements (49 CFR Parts 240 & 242)

## Purpose

This document specifies the system-level regulatory requirements for locomotive engineer
certification (Part 240) and conductor certification (Part 242) that CrewService must
These are **federal law** — they apply to all railroads, all tenants.

---

## 1. Scope: Who Must Be Certified

### Locomotive Engineers — 49 CFR Part 240

**§240.1**: Prescribes minimum Federal safety standards for eligibility, training, testing,
certification, and monitoring of all locomotive engineers.

**§240.7**: A "locomotive engineer" is any person who moves a locomotive or group of
locomotives, regardless of job classification title. Exceptions:
- Moving a locomotive within a repair/servicing area (49 CFR 218.5, 218.29(a)(1))
- Moving a locomotive less than 100 feet for inspection/maintenance

**§240.3**: Applies to all railroads except plant railroads, tourist/scenic/historic operations
not part of the general system, and urban rapid transit not connected to the general system.

### Conductors — 49 CFR Part 242

**§242.1**: Prescribes minimum Federal safety standards for eligibility, training, testing,
certification, and monitoring of all conductors.

**§242.7**: A "conductor" is the crewmember in charge of a train or yard crew, regardless
of job classification title.

**§242.3**: Same railroad applicability scope as Part 240.

**§242.213(d)**: A locomotive engineer operating without an assigned certified conductor must
either hold both engineer and conductor certification, or be accompanied by a certified conductor.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct and Revocation Triggers

### Engineer Prohibited Conduct — §240.305

| # | Violation | Description |
|---|----------|-------------|
| (a)(1) | Signal violation | Operate past a signal requiring complete stop |
| (a)(2) | Excessive speed | Exceed max authorized speed by ≥10 mph |
| (a)(3) | Brake procedure violation | Fail to adhere to brake test procedures (49 CFR 232/238) |
| (a)(4) | Authority violation | Occupy main track without proper authority |
| (a)(5) | Safety device tampering | Tamper with or knowingly operate with disabled safety device |
| (a)(6) | Supervisor/instructor failure | DSLE, pilot, or instructor fails to prevent violations (a)(1)–(5) |

### Conductor Prohibited Conduct — §242.403(e)

| # | Violation | Description |
|---|----------|-------------|
| (e)(1) | Signal violation | Failure to take appropriate action to prevent signal violation |
| (e)(2) | Excessive speed | Failure to take action to prevent exceeding speed by ≥10 mph |
| (e)(3) | Brake procedure violation | Failure to take action re: brake procedures |
| (e)(4) | Authority violation | Failure to take action re: main track authority |
| (e)(5) | Safety device tampering | Failure to take action re: safety devices |
| (e)(6)–(11) | Various operating rule violations | Blue signal, derail, hand brake, shoving, general rules |
| (e)(12) | Failure to perform required brake test | Specific to conductor responsibility |

### Revocation Process — §240.307 / §242.407

| Step | Requirement | CFR |
|------|------------|-----|
| 1 | Immediate suspension upon reliable information | §240.307(b)(1) |
| 2 | Written notice of reason + pending revocation | §240.307(b)(2) — within 96 hours |
| 3 | Hearing before presiding officer (not investigator) | §240.307(b)(3) — within 10 days |
| 4 | Provide written evidence + witness list before hearing | §240.307(b)(4) |
| 5 | Determine on record whether requirements no longer met | §240.307(b)(5) |
| 6 | Impose revocation period per §240.117/§240.119 | §240.307(b)(6) |
| 7 | Retain hearing record for **3 years** | §240.307(b)(7) |

### Substance Abuse Revocation Periods — §240.119 / §242.115

| Violation Count | Part 219 §219.102 (alcohol) | Part 219 §219.101 (drugs) |
|----------------|---------------------------|--------------------------|
| First | Ineligible during evaluation + treatment | Ineligible during evaluation + treatment |
| Second | **2 years** ineligible | **2 years** ineligible |
| Third+ | **Permanent** ineligibility | **Permanent** ineligibility |
| Refusal to test | Treated as single violation | Treated as single violation |

## 4. Certificate Requirements and Monitoring

### Certificate Content — §240.223 / §242.207

The certificate (physical or electronic) must contain:
- Person's name and identification number
- Year of birth
- Type(s) of service certified for
- Name of issuing railroad
- Date of issuance
- Expiration date (max 36 months from issuance)

Temporary replacement certificates valid for **30 days max** (§240.301 / §242.211).

### Classes of Service — §240.107 / §242.107

**Engineer (§240.107)**: A railroad designates at minimum:
- Locomotive service (moving locomotives not attached to trains)
- Train service (operating trains)
- Additional classes may be designated by the railroad

**Conductor (§242.107)**: A railroad designates types of service, which may include:
- Passenger conductor
- Freight conductor
- Yard conductor

### Operational Monitoring — §240.303 / §242.123

| Requirement | Frequency | CFR |
|------------|-----------|-----|
| Operational monitoring observation by qualified supervisor | At least **1 per calendar year** per engineer/conductor | §240.303(b) / §242.123 |
| Unannounced operating rules compliance test | At least **1 per calendar year** per engineer/conductor | §240.303(c) |
| Test results recorded on certificate | Within **30 days** | §240.303(d)(4) |

### Multiple Certifications — §242.213

- A person **may hold both** conductor and engineer certification
- A person may hold certification for **multiple types** of conductor service
- If certification is revoked for one type, **all conductor certifications** are revoked for the revocation period
- If conductor cert revoked for violations (e)(1)–(5) or (e)(12), engineer cert is **also revoked**

## 6. CrewService Data Model Requirements

All entities below are **system-level compliance processes** — the certification lifecycle,
monitoring requirements, revocation procedures, and eligibility checks are enforced by
the system for every covered employee across all railroads and parents. No railroad
configures these rules; they are federal law. The only per-railroad decision is which
crafts/positions require which certifications (via `CraftRegulatoryQualification`).
